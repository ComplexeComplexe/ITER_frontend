# Paris, Fractional CFO and transition: third batch

The Paris and startup pages used bespoke templates and repeated much of the general DAF offer. Paris asserted privileged investor relationships without an identified source. Startup copy promised a standard setup time and salary savings while using availability and pricing inconsistent with the validated offer. Both pages now use the existing French finance-service template and retain their canonical URLs and historical fragment IDs.

- `/daf-externalise-paris`: local intent in title/H1, practical on-site/remote coordination, responsibilities and qualification inputs. No invented Paris street address, opening hours, named local team or investor introductions. The existing Sébastien Doat attribution is retained. Opti Digital is explicitly a cabinet reference, not an asserted Paris client. Real EN/ES Paris alternates remain reciprocal.
- `/fractional-cfo-startups`: title/H1 centred on Fractional CFO, with startup/SaaS cash, assumptions, recurring revenue and board reporting. No standard percentage savings, automatic funding or fixed setup guarantee. Retainer ranges come from the validated facts. The former handwritten ProfessionalService/OfferCatalog and duplicate FAQ lists are replaced by the shared Service/WebPage/FAQ data. Only the FR self and default alternates are emitted, with no synthetic EN/ES startup translations. SolarMente is identified as a cleantech case, not a SaaS case or forecast.
- `/daf-externalise/transition`: natural wording for management de transition finance, written responsibilities, milestones, duration/prolongation conditions and passation. This is not a fabricated client case and does not guarantee a duration or operational deadline. No redirect added; the existing comparison article already links to the transition service and remains published.
- `/services`: direct startup-offer entry in the financial-direction group. Startup resources distinguish the job journey from the commercial offer; the historical job-route redirect to the commercial canonical remains functional.

The legacy French-only Paris component/data files are retired. Their snapshots are in the audit artifacts and Git history. Shared additions are optional typed fields; existing service pages keep their default author, calendar heading and rendering. No client boundary, animation, chart library or new image is introduced.
