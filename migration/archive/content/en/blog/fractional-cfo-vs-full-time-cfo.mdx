# Fractional CFO vs Full-time CFO: Which Option is Right for Your Company?

## 1. Context: Why This Question?

In 2026, many SMEs and scale-ups face a critical decision: hire a full-time CFO or engage a fractional CFO? It's a strategic choice that impacts your costs, flexibility, and ability to scale.

**2026 Trend:** 62% of SMEs now choose a fractional CFO. Reasons: cost-effectiveness, flexibility, and guaranteed seniority.

## 2. Total Cost Comparison

Let's look at the numbers for a typical €8M revenue company (typical growing SME):

| Cost Element | Full-time CFO | Fractional CFO | Notes |
|--------|------------|--------|---|
| **Base Salary** | €50-65k | €0 | - |
| **Social Charges** (42%) | €21-27k | €0 | - |
| **Recruitment Fee** | €3-5k | €0 | Headhunter costs |
| **Training/Certifications** | €1-2k/year | €0 | Turnover risk |
| **Fractional CFO Service** | €0 | €30-50k | 2-3 days/week |
| **In-house Bookkeeper** | €20-30k | €20-30k | Same in both models |
| **External Accounting Firm** | €15-25k | €10-15k | Less control with in-house CFO |
| **Finance Tools (Software)** | €5-10k | €5-10k | Same |
| **TOTAL ANNUAL** | **€115-164k** | **€65-105k** | **-35% to -50% savings** |

## 3. Full-time CFO: Advantages and Drawbacks

**✅ Advantages**
- Physical presence: Available at all times, fluid communication, shared company culture
- Deep domain expertise: After 6-12 months, understands your business intimately
- Team leadership: Can manage and develop accounting/finance team
- Lower marginal cost if stable: If needs remain constant, lower total cost
- Loyalty: Committed to long-term growth

**❌ Drawbacks**
- Turnover risk: Unexpected resignations, 2-3 month replacement timeline
- Limited seniority: At €50-65k, you get 5-10 years of experience, not 15-20
- Fixed costs: You pay full salary regardless of workload fluctuations
- Training costs: Continuous spending on certifications and development
- Less objective perspective: Harder to bring critical, independent viewpoint

## 4. Fractional CFO: Advantages and Drawbacks

**✅ Advantages**
- Controlled and flexible costs: Pay by the day/month. Need more? Add a day.
- Guaranteed seniority: 15-20 years of experience, advanced certifications, networks
- Zero recruitment/turnover risk: Replaced within 48 hours if needed
- Multi-industry expertise: Sees 100+ companies/year, benchmarks, best practices
- Fast implementation: Operational in 2-4 weeks
- Scalable: Adjust days based on fundraising, audits, acquisitions

**❌ Drawbacks**
- Not 100% physically present: Maximum 2-3 days/week on-site
- Less company culture integration: Less emotionally invested
- Potential underperformance: If poorly structured, becomes "distant contractor"
- Possible personnel changes: Rare, but CFO rotation can happen

## 6. Decision Matrix

**✅ Choose a Full-time CFO if:**
- Your revenue has been stable and predictable for 5+ years (€10M+)
- You have a team of 2-3 accounting/finance professionals to supervise
- You have high operational complexity
- You want a 100% integrated strategic partner

**✅ Choose a Fractional CFO if:**
- You're in rapid growth (revenue changes 50%+ annually)
- Your revenue is €2-50M (the sweet spot for fractional CFOs)
- You have a lean accounting team (0-1 person)
- You have limited budget but need senior expertise
- You're preparing for fundraising or acquisition

**⚠️ Choose a Hybrid Model if:**
- Revenue €20-100M+, stable but complex growth
- 1 in-house accountant + 1 fractional CFO (2-3 days/week)
- Optimal balance: internal operations + external senior expertise

## Conclusion

There's no one-size-fits-all answer. Here's the quick summary:

| Company Size/Stage | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|
| **€2-8M, rapid growth** | → Fractional CFO |
| **€10-30M, stable** | → Full-time CFO + accounting firm |
| **€5-50M, all contexts** | → Hybrid model (in-house + fractional CFO) |
